    Calculation notes and assumptions

    Next-shot costs use a historical PGA TOUR distance-and-lie reference for every handicap. Club capacities, shot spreads and woodland severity are teaching assumptions. The model does not explicitly simulate the next shot around obstacles or short-side recovery difficulty. Stable calculations do not prove real-world accuracy.
